iPhone app

The app is written in Swift with SwiftUI and Apple’s CoreBluetooth framework, and it targets iOS 27. Source is in the app folder.

Screenshots

Controls

  • Bluetooth. Connect and Disconnect with a colored indicator. On every connection the app and the RP4 return to the defaults: Manual mode, clockwise, 20 RPM, stopped and not calibrated.
  • Motor. Start and stop, direction, faster and slower.
  • Desired speed. An RPM field that rejects values outside 10 to 100 with a red warning, and an over-range unlock that switches off by itself when the motor stops.
  • Mode. Manual or Maintain.
  • Position. Calibrate zero, set to zero and angle target. The last two are dimmed until the RP4 reports that it is calibrated.
  • Logging. A toggle with a label field, a Mark button, and the file name and elapsed time reported by the RP4.
  • Status. IR RPM, encoder RPM, encoder angle, calibration, motor state (running, moving, holding), lost steps, hold-correction count and Maintain re-syncs, plus a Messages list.

The RP4 is the source of truth: the app shows what the RP4 reports. For example, the over-range unlock turns itself off when the RP4 stops the motor.

Bluetooth link

The RP4 is a Bluetooth Low Energy peripheral that advertises as PARMCO3. We use Bluetooth Low Energy because iOS apps cannot use classic Bluetooth serial. The RP4 side uses BlueZ through the Binc library, and the pairing is “Just Works”, which the iPhone remembers.

Commands

Commands are short lines of text. The RP4 prints each one as [phone] … and answers ACK or ERR.

CommandEffect
PINGConnect handshake; the RP4 stops the motor and restores the defaults
START, STOPRun at the desired rate; stop, disable the H-bridge, cancel any move and switch the unlock off
DIR CW|CCWDirection
FASTER, SLOWERChange the rate by 3%
RPM nDesired RPM, 10 to 100, or above 100 only with the unlock on
MODE MANUAL|MAINTAINSelect the mode
CAL, HOME, ANGLE degCalibrate zero; set to zero; move to an angle. The last two are refused until calibrated
LOG ON [label], LOG OFF, MARK textLogging and event marks
UNLOCK ON|OFFOver-range unlock
BYEThe app sends this and waits before disconnecting, because iOS can keep the link open after the app cancels

The full message format is in protocol.md.

Installing

Open app/PARMCO.xcodeproj in Xcode, choose your team under Signing and Capabilities, select the iPhone and press Run. Free signing lasts about seven days, so we reinstall the app before each check.
