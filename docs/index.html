PARMCO: a phone-controlled stepper motor

An iPhone app talks over Bluetooth Low Energy to a Raspberry Pi 4, which drives a Nema17 stepper motor with a propeller through an H-bridge. An IR sensor, and from Checkpoint 3 an AS5600 magnetic encoder, measure what the propeller is really doing.

Control from the phone

Start, stop, direction, faster/slower, desired RPM (10–100), Manual or Maintain mode, calibrate zero and angle targets, and logging, all from a SwiftUI app.

Drive in assembly

The H-bridge step routine and the step-timing loop are ARM assembly on the Pi, with every GPIO access through the class library libE4235.

Measured, not assumed

Speed is checked independently of the step commands: the IR sensor times whole propeller revolutions, and the encoder will report angle and speed directly.
