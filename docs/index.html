PARMCO: Phone App RP4 Motor Control

An iPhone app that controls a stepper motor and propeller through a Raspberry Pi 4 over Bluetooth, with speed measured by an IR sensor and angle measured by a magnetic encoder.

What it does

PARMCO is the semester project for ECSE 4235. A Nema17 stepper motor turns a two-blade propeller. A Raspberry Pi 4 (RP4) drives the motor through an SN754410NE H-bridge, using an ARM assembly routine for the step timing. An iPhone app talks to the RP4 over Bluetooth Low Energy and is the only way the motor is controlled at the Checkpoint 3 check. The RP4 measures the propeller speed with an IR sensor and the shaft angle with an AS5600 encoder, and it can log everything to a CSV file for analysis.

Control from the phone

Start and stop, direction, faster and slower, a desired RPM from 10 to 100, calibrate zero, set to zero, angle targets, and Manual or Maintain mode.

Two ways to measure

An IR sensor counts blades for RPM. A magnetic encoder reads the shaft angle to 0.088°, which is ten times finer than one half-step (0.9°).

Logging

A 200 Hz CSV log with the specification’s 13 columns, started and stopped from the app, with a Mark button for events.
